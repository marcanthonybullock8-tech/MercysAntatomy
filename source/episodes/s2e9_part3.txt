Amond's gravy boat in the place of honor. C: ALL| Amen.

C: HARMONY| (beat, quietly)| I'm thankful for everybody who's somebody's friend.

C: ESTHER| (beat)| I'm thankful for a Chief who doesn't let people eat alone.

C: NATASHA| (beat)| I'm thankful my daughter's son decided he's not in a hurry. (beat) And for Dr. Hartwell, who called me when he was told not to.

C: MARC| (beat)| I'm thankful for the memories. (beat) All of them. (beat) Even the ones I'd take back.
A: Esther looks at her plate. Victor raises his glass.
C: VICTOR| (beat)| To the memories. (beat) May we make enough good ones to outnumber the rest.
C: ALL| To the memories.
