C: YOUNG MARC| (quietly, as if he's done this before)| Mama. (beat) Mama, wake up. (beat) You have to go to bed.

C: NATASHA| (slurring, then not)| Marc. (beat) Oh, God. (beat) Marc.
C: MARC (V.O.)| A day so big that every day after it is shaped around the hole it left.
