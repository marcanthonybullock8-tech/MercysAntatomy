X: ACT THREE

H: INT. QUEENS GRACE HOSPITAL - STAIRWELL C - NIGHT
T: SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 2006 - 6:47 PM
A: The baby lies on Elxa's bare chest, slippery and pink now, crying in outraged bursts. Harmony has tied the cord with two shoelaces from her own sneakers and cut it with the bandage scissors from her pocket. She pulls off her scrub top, leaving her in a T-shirt, and drapes it over both of them. Then she takes her white coat from under Elxa and wraps the baby in it, folding the long sleeves around him, tucking the edges in tight like a swaddle.
C: HARMONY| (softly, tucking the last corner)| There. (beat) Tucked in. (beat) Like a burrito.
A: Elxa looks down at the furious little face peering out of a white coat that says "H. DIVINE, M.D." over the pocket.
C: ELXA| (whispering, crying)| Look at you. (beat) Tucked in a surgeon's coat. (beat) Tuck. (beat) Hi, Tuck.
C: HARMONY| (beat, laughing through tears)| Tuck?
C: ELXA| (beat)| His name is Russell. (beat) Like his daddy. (beat) Russell Jeffries, Junior. (beat) But we're going to call him Tuck.
A: The baby stops crying. He opens his eyes and looks up at his mother with the unfocused, serious stare of the very new.
C: ELXA| (beat, to Harmony, not taking her eyes off the baby)| And his middle name is Harmon.
A: Harmony stops breathing.
C: HARMONY| (barely)| What?
C: ELXA| (beat)| Russell Harmon Jeffries Junior. (beat) After the woman who caught him in a stairwell during a bomb on Super Bowl Sunday with a pair of shoelaces. (beat) Don't argue with me, Divine. (beat) I've had a long day.
A: Harmony sits back on her heels on the concrete step and sobs. From the phone on the step, Dr. Hampton's voice, thick with tears.
C: DR. HAMPTON (V.O.)| (on the phone)| Twenty-eight years. (beat) That's the first one I ever delivered by phone. (beat) And I didn't do a thing.

H: INT. QUEENS GRACE HOSPITAL - CVICU - BED 5 - NIGHT
T: SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 2006 - 6:49 PM
A: The CVICU lights have flickered back. The TV shows the Super Bowl, first quarter, the sound off. Dash sits up rigid in the bed, his monitor alarming: heart rate one forty. Glenda holds his shoulders. Raven stands frozen in the middle of the room with her cell phone at her ear. Every phone in the building has been turned back on.
C: RAVEN| (into the phone, barely)| Amond. (beat) Amond, the building shook. (beat) Is he... (beat) Is Marc...
A: She listens. Her whole body sags. She grabs the bed rail.
C: RAVEN| (beat, a sob)| He's alive. (beat) He's alive. (beat) A cut. (beat) His hearing. (beat) He's alive.
A: Dash lets out a long breath. On the monitor, his heart rate begins to fall: one thirty, one twenty, one ten. Glenda closes her eyes.
C: RAVEN| (beat, into the phone)| And the sergeant? (beat) The bomb squad...
A: She listens. She closes her eyes.
C: RAVEN| (barely)| Oh, no.
C: DASH| (quietly)| Go, Raven. (beat) Go to your friend.
C: RAVEN| (beat)| I promised you the game.
C: DASH| (beat)| You promised me "when he walks out." (beat) He hasn't walked out yet. (beat) Go make him walk out. (beat) Then come back for the third quarter.

H: INT. QUEENS GRACE HOSPITAL - NEURO ICU - BED 2 - NIGHT
T: SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 2006 - 6:51 PM
A: The Neuro ICU, two floors and a whole wing from the blast. A cracked ceiling tile hangs by one corner over the nurses' station. Esther stands at Russell's bedside where she threw herself across his bandaged head twenty minutes ago, when the floor jumped. She's still shaking. Graves comes in fast, out of breath.
C: GRAVES| (beat)| Pressure?
C: ESTHER| (beat, reading the monitor)| ICP spiked to twenty-eight when the building shook. (beat) He bucked the vent. (beat) I pushed propofol and he came back down. (beat) Fourteen now. (beat) Twelve.
C: GRAVES| (beat, checking the bolt himself)| Good. (beat) Good catch, Dr. Smilley.
A: Esther looks at the cracked ceiling tile, then out the window toward the old wing, where smoke still curls under the floodlights.
C: ESTHER| (barely)| Dr. Graves. (beat) Marc Bullock was in OR One.
C: GRAVES| (beat, quietly)| I know. (beat) Big Ray called the charge nurse five minutes ago. (beat) Bullock's alive. (beat) A cut and his ears. (beat) The sergeant isn't.
A: Esther grips the bed rail. She closes her eyes. She doesn't let go of Russell's hand.
C: GRAVES| (beat, gently)| Stay with this one, Dr. Smilley. (beat) The other one has his family. (beat) This one only has you until his wife can get here.

H: INT. QUEENS GRACE HOSPITAL - OR 1 - NIGHT
T: SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 2006 - 6:53 PM
A: Smoke still hangs in the air near the east door, and dust coats every surface. A ceiling tile has fallen onto the back table. But Reggie's abdomen is closed. Lowery ties the last skin suture. Mabry, still bagging by hand, glances at the monitor. Steady. Then Lowery lifts his hands from the field and holds them out in front of him. They're shaking. Violently.
C: MABRY| (beat, dry, gentle)| Now you shake?
C: LOWERY| (beat, staring at his hands)| Now I can, Edwin. (beat) Now I'm allowed.
A: He lowers his hands slowly to his sides. He looks at the east door, at the dust, at the smoke curling under it.
C: LOWERY| (beat, very quietly)| He walked right past me. (beat) That sergeant. (beat) Carrying it. (beat) He said, "Thank you, Doctor." (beat) I didn't say anything back. (beat) I was looking at the wound.
C: MABRY| (beat)| That was your job, Abraham.
C: LOWERY| (beat)| I know. (beat) I'd still like to have said something.

H: EXT. QUEENS GRACE HOSPITAL - JACKSON AVENUE - POLICE PERIMETER - NIGHT
T: SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 2006 - 6:56 PM
A: Chaos at the tape. People are crying and pushing at the barricades. Police shout. Smoke curls from the loading dock area at the side of the old wing. Ambulances pull up with their lights on. Victor stands perfectly still at the barricade, gripping it with both hands. Lillian holds Camille against her. LaShawn holds her toddler and sobs. Big Ray, just inside the tape, has a radio pressed to his ear.
C: BIG RAY| (into the radio)| Say again. (beat) Say again, all of it.
A: He listens. He lowers the radio. He turns to the crowd at the tape, finds Victor, and speaks directly to him.
C: BIG RAY| (beat)| The doctors are alive, Dr. Donohue. (beat) All of them. (beat) The patient's alive. (beat) Marc's alive. (beat) He's cut. He can't hear right. (beat) But he's alive.
A: Victor's grip on the barricade loosens. His knees bend. Cedric catches him under one arm, Monty under the other, and they lower the seventy-two-year-old man gently to sit on the curb in his overcoat. Victor puts his face in his hands.
C: LASHAWN| (to Big Ray, frantic)| My husband?
C: BIG RAY| (beat)| Alive, Mrs. Plummer. (beat) They closed him up. (beat) He's alive.
A: LaShawn sinks to her knees on the wet sidewalk with her son. Big Ray looks back at the radio in his hand. His face changes.
C: BIG RAY| (beat, quietly, to nobody)| The sergeant didn't make it.

H: INT. MONTY'S ON JACKSON - BAR - NIGHT
T: SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 2006 - 7:02 PM
A: The bar is nearly empty; everyone is across the street at the tape. Only Kendra Wallace, a few nurses and an old regular remain, all standing at the window. On the TV, the Super Bowl plays on: first quarter, Seattle driving. Nobody watches. Behind the bar, a framed photo of the Grace's 1998 softball team has fallen off the wall and shattered from the blast.
A: Kendra picks up the broken frame. In the photo, a younger Gloria Pettiford in a softball jersey grins beside a younger Monty.
C: KENDRA| (beat, to the old regular)| It shook the glasses. (beat) From across the street.
C: OLD REGULAR| (beat, quietly)| I was in Brooklyn in '93 when the Trade Center got hit the first time. (beat) That's what this felt like. (beat) Like the ground had a heart attack.

H: INT. QUEENS GRACE HOSPITAL - EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT - TRAUMA 3 - NIGHT
T: SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 2006 - 7:08 PM
A: Marc sits on the edge of a gurney, still in his bloody scrubs, dust in his hair. Gloria cleans the cut above his eyebrow with saline and closes it with Steri-Strips. Natasha stands on one side of him, Robert on the other. Marc's hearing is coming back in pieces: muffled voices, a hiss, a high whine.
C: GLORIA| (holding up an otoscope, speaking slowly and loudly)| Your eardrums are intact, baby. (beat) Both of them. (beat) Temporary threshold shift. (beat) It'll come back in a day or two. (beat) The ringing might take a week.
C: MARC| (too loudly)| I can hear you. (beat) Sort of. (beat) Like you're underwater.
C: NATASHA| (taking his hand)| That's enough for tonight.
A: A tall, gray-haired NYPD officer in uniform with captain's bars comes into the bay with his hat under his arm: CAPTAIN NEVILLE ASHFORD (56), commander of the Bomb Squad.
C: ASHFORD| (beat, formally)| Dr. Bullock. (beat) I'm Captain Ashford. (beat) Isaac Burrell was my sergeant for eleven years. (beat) I'm told you held that device for four hours. (beat) And handed it to him.
C: MARC| (beat)| Yes, sir.
C: ASHFORD| (beat)| He radioed from the corridor, before. (beat) The last thing he said on the radio was, "The doctor's clear. Starting my walk." (beat) You were his last report, son. (beat) He was proud of how you held it.
A: Marc looks down at his hands. They've stopped shaking. He doesn't know whether that's better or worse.
C: MARC| (beat)| Captain. (beat) When you take him out of the building... (beat) I want to be there.

A: Danita sits where Kennerly and Arianna left her, on the landing, her knees pulled up, hugging her cramped hand. Kennerly comes back down the stairs with two cups of water. He sits on the step below her and hands her one. She doesn't take it.
C: DANITA| (beat, barely)| The building shook.
C: KENNERLY| (beat)| I know.
C: DANITA| (beat)| Did it go off? (beat) Inside? (beat) With the doctor?
C: KENNERLY| (beat)| In the corridor. (beat) After. (beat) The doctor's alive. (beat) The patient's alive.
A: A long beat. Danita looks at him.
C: DANITA| (beat, whispering)| Who?
A: Kennerly sets her cup of water down on the step between them.
C: KENNERLY| (beat, honestly)| The bomb squad sergeant. (beat) Burrell. (beat) He was carrying it out. (beat) He's dead.

H: EXT. QUEENS GRACE HOSPITAL - LOADING DOCK - NIGHT
T: SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 2006 - 7:24 PM
A: Floodlights. The loading dock is scorched and the doors are gone. An NYPD ambulance waits in the alley with its back doors open. Along both sides of the path from the dock to the ambulance, two long lines of bomb squad officers and uniformed police stand at attention in the cold. Behind them, more and more hospital staff in scrubs and white coats join the lines: nurses, security guards, cafeteria workers, residents. Miss Hattie. Big Ray. Mabry. Lowery, his hands now still.
A: At the end of one line, in bloody scrubs, a white bandage over his eyebrow, Marc stands between his father and his mother.
A: Six officers carry a stretcher out of the loading dock. On it, a body covered with an American flag and, on top of the flag, a single NYPD Bomb Squad patch. They walk slowly between the lines. Every officer salutes. Every hospital worker stands still. Some put their hands over their hearts.
A: As the stretcher passes Marc, he raises his right hand, the hand that held the shell, and lays it flat over his own heart.
C: MARC (V.O.)| They teach you in medical school that the body has its own wisdom.
A: The officers lift the stretcher into the ambulance and close the doors. The ambulance pulls away slowly, its lights on and no siren, down the alley toward Jackson Avenue. Nobody moves until it's gone.

H: INT. QUEENS GRACE HOSPITAL - STAIRWELL C - NIGHT
T: SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 2006 - 7:33 PM
A: The lockdown has been lifted. With a mechanical clunk, the fire door at the bottom of the flight unlocks. It bursts open. Clayton runs through it with a NICU nurse and a transport team. Behind them, taking the stairs two at a time in a soaked raincoat, comes Dr. Hampton.

C: HARMONY| (beat, hoarse)| She did it. (beat) I caught.

C: DR. HAMPTON| (beat)| Shoelaces?
C: HARMONY| (beat)| They were the only thing I had that would tie.
C: DR. HAMPTON| (beat, laughing and crying at once)| Lord, child. (beat) Shoelaces.

C: ELXA| (beat, looking up, exhausted, radiant)| Mama. (beat) Meet Tuck.
A: Natasha sits down on the step above her daughter. She reaches over Elxa's shoulder and touches the baby's cheek with one finger. Then she puts her face in her hands and weeps, for the first time all day.

A: Marc walks slowly back in from the loading dock, Robert's hand on his shoulder. At the end of the corridor, running: Raven, Amond, Arianna and Esther. They reach him. C: AMOND| (beat, quietly)| You stayed in a room you couldn't leave.
C: MARC| (beat, too loud, still half deaf)| What?
A: Amond takes Marc's face in both hands, the way Robert did in the scrub room, and speaks slowly enough for him to read his lips.
C: AMOND| (beat)| You call me your day one. (beat) I never told you what you are to me. (beat) You're not my day one, Marc. (beat) You're my brother.
A: Marc stares at him. Then he puts his forehead against Amond's. Amond, who doesn't hug people, wraps his arms around him and holds on.
C: AMOND| (beat, barely)| My father bled out before anybody got there. (beat) Today you were the person in the room who knew what to do. (beat) Today you got there.
